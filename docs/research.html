DigA-kog: Digital Screening for Early Detection of Mild Cognitive Impairment
Data collection ongoing 

MCI / Dementia Digital Assessment OCS-Plus Normative Study Multi-centre Validation

Multi-centre validation study evaluating a self-administered digital cognitive screening tool for the early detection of mild cognitive impairment, including normative data collection and wearable integration.

Early Detection of Memory Problems in Amnestic Mild Cognitive Impairment
Analysis and write-up 

MCI / Dementia Digital Assessment OCS-Plus Clinical Validation

Validation study examining the OCS-Plus tablet-based test for the detection of amnestic mild cognitive impairment, with a focus on delayed memory as an early marker of Alzheimer’s-related change.

Automated Cognitive Screening: Development of the MEYOCS-plus
Ongoing 

MCI / Dementia Parkinson’s Disease Liver Disease OCS-Plus MEYOCS-plus Digital Assessment Clinical Validation

Neuropsychological development of a fully automated adaptation of the OCS-Plus tablet test, in collaboration with industry, enabling self-administered cognitive screening without clinical staff. Now the basis of an independent validation and implementation study in rural healthcare settings.

MS-Children: Neurodevelopment After Prenatal Exposure to MS Treatment
Ongoing 

Children / Neurodevelopment Foetal Programming Multi-centre MRI / Neuroimaging

Cross-sectional two-centre study examining whether prenatal exposure to methylprednisolone used to treat MS relapses during pregnancy affects cognitive development, behaviour, and brain structure in school-aged children.

Digital Neuropsychological Assessment: A Scoping Review
Ongoing
Neurocognitive disorders Digital Assessment Review

Systematic scoping review mapping the landscape of digital neuropsychological tests for neurocognitive disorders, examining psychometric quality, normative data availability, and clinical applicability across the published literature.
